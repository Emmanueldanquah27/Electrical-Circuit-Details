OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
The project studies an extension board that distributes mains power to multiple outlets and provides USB charging and voltage monitoring. Objectives: identify components, explain their functions and operation, and understand safe inspection and testing.

COMPONENTS & FUNCTIONS
1. Mains plug and supply cable: bring mains power into the board.
2. Enclosure: houses and protects internal connections.
3. Socket outlets: connect electrical appliances to the supply.
4. Switches: control power to the corresponding outlet or section.
5. Indicator lights: show the power/operating status of a section.
6. USB-A port: supplies low-voltage DC for compatible device charging.
7. USB Type-C port: supplies low-voltage DC for compatible device charging.
8. USB power module: converts mains AC to regulated low-voltage DC.
9. Digital voltage display: shows the voltage supplied to the extension board.
10.Circuit breaker: it trips on and off to protect the board from overload  and damage of equipment 

OPERATING PRINCIPLE
Mains power enters through the plug and cable. Internal wiring distributes it to the socket outlets. Switches control the relevant sections and indicator lights show their status. A USB power module converts mains AC into regulated low-voltage DC for the USB-A and USB Type-C ports. The digital meter monitors and displays the incoming supply voltage.

INSPECTION & TESTING
Testing must be done by a competent person under workshop supervision.
• Unplug before visual inspection.
• Check the plug, cable, casing, sockets, switches and ports for damage or loose parts.
• With the board unplugged, perform appropriate continuity and protective-earth checks using suitable equipment.
• A competent person should perform insulation-resistance, polarity and other required safety tests.
• Only after safety tests pass, energise under supervision.
• Check the voltage display, switches, indicators, outlets and USB charging operation. Record results. Do not use the board if a test fails.

SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
• Never open or repair the board while connected to mains.
• Keep it dry; never handle it with wet hands.
• Do not use damaged plugs, cables, casing or sockets.
• Do not overload it; follow its rated voltage and current.
• Never insert metal objects into sockets or ports.
• Use correctly rated test instruments and insulated leads.
• Mains testing and repairs must be done by a qualified person or under competent supervision.


CONCLUSION
The extension board combines multiple socket outlets, switched control, status indicators, USB-A and USB Type-C charging, and a digital display of incoming supply voltage. Studying it develops knowledge of power distribution, electrical testing and safe equipment use.
